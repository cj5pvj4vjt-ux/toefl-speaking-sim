8-11 As part of a university course, you are taking part in a short online interview about travel habits. A researcher will ask you some questions.
8.Thank you for joining us. First, can you tell me how often you travel and what kind of places you like to visit?
9.Describe a trip you took that you still remember clearly. What made it memorable?
10.If you could change one thing about the way people travel today, what would it be and why?
11.Some people say that travelling abroad is the best way to learn about other cultures. Do you agree or disagree? Why?
【文】8. I try to travel two or three times a year. I prefer small towns and nature parks rather than big cities, because they are quieter and cheaper.
9. I still remember a trip to a mountain village last spring. The views were amazing and the local people were very welcoming.
10. I would make train travel cheaper and more convenient, so that fewer people would choose short flights.
11. I partly agree. Travelling abroad does teach you a lot, but you can also learn about other cultures by reading and by talking to people at home.
